Design Notes

  • Vertical path — connected nodes with a fading trail, giving a sense of progression
  • Destination node — green flag at the end showing what they'll arrive at (their personalised dashboard)
  • During onboarding — this same map appears as a mini sidebar, with completed steps turning green with a tick, current step pulsing, and upcoming steps greyed
  • Gradient fade — the connector line and node shadows gradually fade, drawing the eye from start to finish
  • Mobile — same layout works perfectly as a scrollable vertical path
