What Changed

  • Net Worth card — now shows a stacked asset bar, breakdown by category (property, pensions, investments, savings), plus total assets vs liabilities
  • Protection card — shows each cover type (life, critical illness, income protection) with adequacy badges. Inline action: "Close your critical illness gap"
  • Mortgage card — shows remaining vs property value, Loan to Value bar, monthly payment, rate, years, equity. Inline action: "Overpay £200/month to save £18,400"
  • Retirement card — shows current vs projected value, pension breakdown per person, contribution amount, State Pension estimate, Annual Allowance usage. Inline action: "Increase contribution by 2%"
  • Investments card — shows portfolio value, return %, account breakdown, ISA allowance usage, risk profile. Inline action: "Move £4,600 from GIA to ISA"
  • Inline actions — each action sits inside the card it pertains to, with contextual colour (raspberry for urgent, violet for optimisation, spring for growth). Action text includes the specific financial impact
  • Separate "Top Actions" card eliminated — actions now live where they belong
  • Life Timeline — now horizontal (full width at bottom), showing the user's life as a visual timeline from past to future. Imminent events glow in raspberry with "See impact" links
