Goals & Life Events on Dashboard

  • Goals card — shows active goals with progress bars, amounts, and timelines. Stage-suggested goals appear as dashed "Suggested" cards at the bottom
  • Life Timeline card — vertical timeline showing past events (green dots), current/imminent events (stage-coloured with glow), and planned future events (grey). Imminent events link to "See how this affects your plan"
  • "What if" prompt — stage-aware scenario suggestions at the bottom of the timeline card. Links to the existing What If Scenarios feature
  • "+ Add goal" / "+ Add event" — quick-add CTAs in raspberry, always visible in card headers
  • All colours from fynlaDesignGuide.md v1.2.0 — raspberry-500 for CTAs and gaps, violet-500 for info/progress, spring-500 for success/completion, horizon-500 for text, eggshell-500 for backgrounds, neutral-500 for secondary text, light-gray for borders
  • Font — Segoe UI primary as per design guide
