Journey Preview — "Starting Out" (Student Stage)
🎓
Starting Out
Build smart money habits from day one
6 steps · About 10 minutes
Here's what we'll cover and why it matters
1
About You
Your age and situation shape everything — from student loan repayment thresholds to when you'll be auto-enrolled in a pension. This helps us tailor all guidance to your actual circumstances.
2
Your Student Loan
Student loans in the UK aren't like normal debt — repayments are income-based and written off after 30-40 years. Understanding your plan type helps us show you the real cost and when it makes sense to overpay (spoiler: usually it doesn't).
3
Your Income
Whether it's a part-time job, placement salary, or parental support — knowing what comes in each month is the foundation of budgeting. We'll use this to build you a realistic spending plan.
4
Your Spending
Most students overspend by £400-600 a month without realising. Tracking your spending isn't about restriction — it's about knowing where your money goes so you can make conscious choices.
5
Your Savings
Even £25 a month builds a safety net. We'll set up your accounts — whether it's a Cash ISA, a Lifetime ISA for your future first home, or just a basic savings account. Starting now is what matters most.
6
Your Goals
Graduate debt-free (beyond your student loan)? Build an emergency fund? Save for a car? Setting clear goals now gives your money a purpose and keeps you motivated.

Key Details

  • "Start My Journey" — for signed-up users, begins the onboarding questions

  • Step descriptions are educational and encouraging, not just listing what data we collect
  • The "You can skip steps" note reduces anxiety about commitment
