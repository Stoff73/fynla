Section 6b: Goals & Life Events — Stage-Aware

Stage-suggested goals, life events that trigger stage transitions and surface new modules

Three connected concepts

  1. Stage-suggested goals — each life stage surfaces relevant goals the user might not think of
  2. Life events — material changes (baby, marriage, redundancy, inheritance) that reshape the user's financial picture
  3. Stage evolution — life events can trigger the app to suggest transitioning to a new stage, surfacing new modules and guidance

How It All Connects

  • Stage-suggested goals — when user enters the Goals section, they see goals relevant to their stage. They can add custom goals too, but the suggestions give them a starting point
  • Life events as triggers — adding a life event (baby, marriage, house purchase, redundancy, inheritance, retirement) triggers the lifeStageConfig to re-evaluate:
    • Which modules should be promoted to the primary sidebar
    • Which dashboard cards should appear
    • Which actions become high priority
    • Whether a stage transition should be suggested
  • Stage transitions are always optional — user gets a friendly prompt, never forced. They can stay on their current stage or switch
  • Module promotion without stage change — a student who inherits money doesn't need to change stage, but the Investment and Estate modules automatically appear in their primary sidebar
  • Goals persist across stages — if a user transitions from "Building Foundations" to "Protecting What Matters", their existing goals carry over
