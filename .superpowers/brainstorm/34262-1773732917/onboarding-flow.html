Did you know?
Student loans are not like normal debt. You only repay when earning above the threshold, and the balance is written off after 40 years (Plan 5).
Most graduates will never fully repay — it works more like a graduate tax than a traditional loan.
Why we ask this
Your plan type determines your repayment threshold (£25k for Plan 5) and interest rate. This affects whether overpaying ever makes financial sense — for most people, it doesn't.
How this fits your journey
Understanding your loan means you won't waste money overpaying when that money could be building your emergency fund or going into a Lifetime ISA instead.

Design Details

  • Progress bar — compact journey map at top with completed (green tick), current (violet pulse), upcoming (grey) steps
  • Left column (60%) — clean form inputs, minimal, focused. Back/Skip/Continue navigation at bottom
  • Right column (360px) — learning sidebar with three sections:
    • "Did you know?" — the learning milestone, educational and engaging
    • "Why we ask this" — explains what this data point does
    • "How this fits your journey" — connects the input to their stage goals
  • Quick stat — a concrete, memorable number that makes the learning tangible
  • Mobile — learning sidebar stacks below the form as a collapsible "Learn more" accordion
  • Each step has unique content — defined in the lifeStageConfig per stage per step
  • "Not sure" options — always available so users never feel stuck
