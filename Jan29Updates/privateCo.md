## 1. Core Investment Details (Non-Negotiable)

These apply to **all** private or crowdfunding investments.

### Identity & Structure

* **Company legal name**
* **Company registration number** (Companies House)
* **Country of incorporation**
* **Registered address**
* **Trading name** (if different)
* **Sector / industry classification**
* **Website**
* **Crowdfunding platform used** (if applicable, e.g. Seedrs, Crowdcube)

### Investment Metadata

* **Investment date**
* **Amount invested (£)**
* **Currency**
* **Investment round** (Seed, Series A, Bridge, SAFE, etc.)
* **Valuation at entry**

  * Pre-money
  * Post-money
* **Price per share / unit**
* **Number of shares / units acquired**
* **Instrument type**

  * Ordinary shares
  * Preference shares
  * Convertible loan note
  * SAFE
  * Revenue share
  * Fund/nominee interest

---

## 2. Ownership & Legal Rights (Critical for Risk & Exit)

### Shareholding Details

* **Share class**
* **Voting rights (yes/no)**
* **Dividend rights**
* **Liquidation preference**
* **Anti-dilution protections**
* **Conversion terms** (for CLNs/SAFEs)
* **Interest rate & maturity** (if debt-like)

### Nominee / Platform Structure

* Is the investment:

  * **Direct shareholding**, or
  * **Held via nominee** (very common in crowdfunding)?
* **Nominee name**
* **Underlying beneficial ownership confirmation**
* **Investor agreement / subscription agreement (PDF)**

⚠️ This matters hugely if the company is sold, restructures, or fails.

---

## 3. Tax & Relief Tracking (UK-Specific, Very Important)

### Tax Relief Eligibility

Store **explicit yes/no flags** and documentation for:

* **EIS** (Enterprise Investment Scheme)
* **SEIS**
* **SITR**
* **VCT** (rare via crowdfunding but possible)
* **No relief**

### Required Tax Documents

* **EIS3 / SEIS3 certificates**
* **HMRC reference numbers**
* **Date relief claimed**
* **Amount of income tax relief claimed**
* **Carry-back year used** (if any)

### Holding Period Rules

* **Minimum holding period** (e.g. 3 years for EIS/SEIS)
* **Disposal restriction date**
* **Clawback risk flag** (yes/no)

---

## 4. Ongoing Performance & Monitoring

Private investments don’t have prices — so *you* must track proxies.

### Company Updates

* **Latest valuation**
* **Last funding round date**
* **Round valuation**
* **Revenue updates**
* **User/customer metrics**
* **Profit/loss status**
* **Runway (months)**
* **Key milestones hit/missed**

### Your Position

* **Current ownership % (if known)**
* **Dilution history**
* **Paper gain/loss**
* **Impairment write-downs** (0–100%)
* **Status flag**

  * Active
  * Distressed
  * Dormant
  * Failed
  * Exited

---

## 5. Exit, Liquidity & Failure Tracking

### Exit Events

* **Exit type**

  * Acquisition
  * Secondary sale
  * Buyback
  * IPO
  * Liquidation
* **Exit date**
* **Gross proceeds**
* **Net proceeds**
* **Fees deducted**
* **Multiple (MOIC)**
* **IRR (optional but powerful)**

### Losses

* **Date of failure**
* **Loss relief eligibility**
* **Capital loss amount**
* **Negligible value claim filed (yes/no)**

---

## 6. Documents You Should Always Store

At minimum, keep **digital copies** of:

* Pitch deck
* Subscription agreement
* Share certificate (or nominee confirmation)
* Shareholders’ agreement
* EIS/SEIS certificates
* Annual statements from platform
* Exit or liquidation notices

---

## 7. Optional (But Highly Valuable) Advanced Fields

If you’re building a serious investor system:

* **Founder names**
* **Board members**
* **Lead investor**
* **Cap table snapshot**
* **Follow-on rights**
* **Pro-rata rights**
* **ESG / impact notes**
* **Risk score**
* **Confidence / conviction rating**
* **Reason for investment (qualitative)**

---

## 8. How This Differs from Public Shares (Key Insight)

| Aspect            | Public Shares | Private / Crowdfunding |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ---------------------- |
| Price             | Continuous    | Event-based            |
| Liquidity         | High          | Very low               |
| Tax reporting     | Simple        | Document-heavy         |
| Ownership clarity | Clear         | Often nominee-based    |
| Valuation         | Market        | Subjective             |
| Failure risk      | Lower         | Much higher            |
