## Rule 20 finding (flagged, not fixed)

The navigation allowlist is maintained in **three** hand-kept copies:

| Source | Routes | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `app/Services/Onboarding/OnboardingStateMachine::campaignVerifyConfig()` | 8 | the verify destinations |
| `resources/mobile/mixins/onboardingChat.js:31` `ONBOARDING_NAV_ROUTES` | 9 | + `/tax-strategy` terminal |
| `ios-native/…/FynEventReducer.swift:160` `allowedRoute` | 9 | + `/tax-strategy` terminal |

All three agree **today** — verified by reading each. But both client copies
carry a comment saying "keep in step with" another source, and Rule 20 names
exactly that: *"editing copies in lockstep is a violation, not a fix."* One
vocabulary, three homes, held together by comment discipline. The failure mode is
silent — a route added server-side simply does nothing on a client that was not
updated, which is the bug `37cd69e` fixed and which will recur.

Not fixed here: the task was a baseline, and consolidating this is a design call.
