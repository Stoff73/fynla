# New: the Fynla Pension Check

*Feature notes — user-facing. 2026-07-04. Live on the dev environment (csjones); not yet released to fynla.org. All wording below is draft pending final copy review.*

## Check where your pension is heading — in about three minutes

There's a new way into Fynla for anyone wondering whether their pension is on track. From the homepage, the **"Where is your pension heading?"** card takes you to a short questionnaire — six quick questions, no account needed:

1. How you work (employed, self-employed, retired…)
2. Roughly what you earn
3. Your age group
4. What pensions you already have — workplace, personal, final salary, or none yet
5. Roughly what's in them
6. Whether you have a spouse

You immediately see a personalised estimate: **the pension pot you're on course for by State Pension age**, the monthly contribution that assumes, and a note on the tax relief you may be missing. Nothing is stored unless you choose to create an account — and if you do, everything you've already answered carries straight through so you're never asked twice.

## A guided pension conversation with Fyn

After registering, Fyn — your financial planning assistant — walks you through the details that make your retirement picture real, one short question at a time:

- Your income, confirmed on screen as it's saved
- Your workplace pension: what you and your employer pay in, and what the pot is worth today
- Any personal pensions or Self-Invested Personal Pensions
- Final salary or career average pensions — the kind that pay a guaranteed income
- Your State Pension forecast, if you know it (and it's fine to say "not sure" — Fyn moves on and reminds you where to check)
- When you'd like to retire, and the yearly income that would feel comfortable
- Your monthly spending, so any suggestions stay affordable

Every answer is saved to your records the moment you give it, read back to you for confirmation, and shown on the relevant page so you can check and correct as you go. At the end, Fyn brings it together:

> **Here's your pension picture, built from what you told me — in the order I'd tackle it:**
> - Pension contributions below the auto-enrolment minimum
> - Increase pension contributions
> - No State Pension forecast entered
> - Consider adjusting retirement age

One tap then takes you to your retirement page, where your pensions, targets and projections live from then on — and each suggestion joins your actions list to come back to any time.

## Already a Fynla member? You won't repeat yourself

If you've already been through Fynla's tax journey, the pension check respects everything you've told us. Fyn opens with a recap —

> "Welcome back. Let's take a proper look at your pension. Here's what I already have from you…"

— and then asks **only what's missing**: typically the value of pensions we knew about but hadn't valued, any final salary schemes, your State Pension forecast, and your retirement goals. In testing, that's around seven questions instead of a full onboarding. Your original journey, its history and your progress are untouched, and when the pension check finishes you're back to normal — ask Fyn anything, any time.

## The details that keep it trustworthy

- Fyn never claims to have saved something it hasn't — if an answer can't be recorded, it says so plainly and asks again.
- "Not sure" is always an acceptable answer; the walk carries on and the gap simply shows on your retirement checklist.
- Every tax figure — allowances, thresholds, relief — comes from the current tax year's official values.
- Your existing Fynla experience is unchanged: the tax journey, your dashboard, your actions and milestones all work exactly as before, and completing the pension check earns progress in the usual way.
