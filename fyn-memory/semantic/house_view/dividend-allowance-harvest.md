## What this is, when it applies, who it is for

The first slice of dividend income each tax year is tax-free under the
Dividend Allowance, whatever band the investor is in. Someone who holds
investments outside an ISA but whose dividend income sits below the allowance
has unused tax-free capacity: income-paying holdings in the taxable account
return their full payout up to that line, with no tax to pay. The strategy
applies when the user has non-ISA investments and recorded dividend income
below the allowance; it is for investors whose taxable holdings tilt towards
growth and who could let the allowance do some work. It is irrelevant once
dividend income already fills the allowance — at that point the question
flips to sheltering the excess, not harvesting headroom.

## Why Fynla quantifies it this way

Fynla measures the headroom as the allowance from the live tax configuration
minus the user's recorded dividend income, and values it at the dividend rate
for the user's band — the tax those dividends would suffer if they were
earned above the allowance instead. That framing keeps the claim honest: the
allowance does not hand the user money, it makes a defined slice of dividend
income tax-free that would otherwise be taxed. Fynla ranks this strategy low,
because the allowance is small beside the pension and ISA moves and the
benefit only accrues if the portfolio actually pays dividends.

## Where it sits in sequence

This strategy sits at the bottom of the plan and conflicts with nothing. It
does not argue against the wrapper-first house view: ISA and pension
sheltering still come first for new money, and the allowance simply means
that holdings which remain outside the wrapper anyway can be arranged so a
slice of their income is tax-free. Once dividend income grows past the
allowance, this strategy retires and the bed-and-ISA and spouse-transfer
strategies take over the job of sheltering the excess.

## Claim tier and voicing

Mechanical tier: the headroom and its value follow from recorded dividend
income, the published allowance, and the band rate, so Fyn states the working
directly. Voicing keeps the modest scale honest — this is a small, tidy
saving rather than a headline move, and Fyn presents it as finishing touches
on a portfolio rather than a reason to hold more outside the wrapper.
