## What this is, when it applies, who it is for

Interest on savings a person gives outright to their spouse or civil partner
is the spouse's income for tax. Moving savings to the partner who pays less
tax on interest can therefore lower the household's tax: the giver pays less,
and the partner pays whatever their own Personal Allowance, Starting Rate for
Savings and Personal Savings Allowance do not cover. Fynla considers this for
every married or civil-partner couple, priced on both partners' whole income.
It waits when the partner's income or savings are not known, because their
remaining tax-free room cannot be assumed. The cash becomes the partner's
property. Transfers between eligible spouses or civil partners usually create
no immediate Capital Gains Tax charge and may qualify for the Inheritance Tax
spouse exemption, subject to the relevant conditions.

## Why Fynla quantifies it this way

Fynla prices both partners on their whole income with the same tax engine, so
each person's allowances are applied as HM Revenue and Customs would. It finds
the amount of interest that saves the household the most, takes the savings
that earn it from the highest-rate accounts first, rounds that down to the
nearest hundred pounds and prices that exact amount again, so the card's
figures agree. The card carries its working: each partner's interest before
and after, what is tax-free and under which allowance, what is taxed at which
rate, and the totals. Fyn presents that working rather than redoing the sums.

## Where it sits in sequence

Wrapping the user's own cash in an ISA and gifting that same cash to a spouse
draw on the same pot and must not be counted twice. An outright gift also
conflicts with placing the same money in a joint account. Money already gifted
to the spouse could later be subscribed to the spouse's ISA only within their
confirmed allowance.

## How firmly to state it

A judgement call because ownership changes and unrecorded partner income would
change the result. Where the partner's income and savings are recorded, Fyn may
present the card's working while describing it as conditional on those facts
remaining complete.
