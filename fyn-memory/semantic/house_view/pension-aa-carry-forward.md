## What this is, when it applies, who it is for

Unused pension Annual Allowance does not vanish at the end of the tax year:
HM Revenue and Customs allows unused allowance from the previous three tax
years to be carried forward and used once the current year's allowance is
spent. Someone who contributed below the allowance in past years can
therefore make a much larger contribution this year with full tax relief.
The strategy applies to higher and additional-rate taxpayers who have
captured their pension contribution history, have not already filled this
year's allowance, and have genuine unused allowance inside the exact
three-year lookback window. Relief is always capped by relevant UK earnings,
so a large carry-forward figure means nothing to someone whose earnings
cannot support the contribution.

## Why Fynla quantifies it this way

Fynla sums the unused allowance year by year across the user's recorded
pension input history, counting only the three tax years the rules actually
allow — stale history outside the window never inflates the figure. The raw
total is then cut down twice before it becomes a recommendation. First, the
tax-relief cap: contributions only attract relief up to gross UK earnings
less what has already gone in this year, so the headline is bounded by what
the user can genuinely get relief on. Second, an affordability gate: Fynla
checks the user's accessible non-ISA cash and recommends no more than a
conservative share of it, so the suggestion never amounts to emptying the
bank account or raiding the emergency fund. The final figure is rounded down,
never up, so the headline cannot overstate, and the tax saved is that
contribution at the user's marginal rate.

## Where it sits in sequence

Carry-forward is the strategy that creates room for the others: the taper
rescue and additional-rate avoidance both need annual allowance headroom, and
carry-forward is where extra headroom comes from, so Fynla establishes the
carry-forward position before sizing those contributions. The tapered annual
allowance warning sits above it for the highest earners, since the taper
shrinks each year's allowance and with it the amount available to carry
forward. It does not conflict with the ISA strategies — but where both
compete for the same cash, relief at the higher marginal rates is the
stronger first claim.

## Claim tier and voicing

Mechanical tier: the unused allowance, the earnings cap, and the relief all
follow from recorded history and published rules, so Fyn states the working
directly — the unused total across the window, why the usable amount is
smaller, and what the recommended contribution saves. Voicing is candid about
the two caps: the user should understand that the headline is deliberately
conservative, bounded by their earnings and their accessible cash rather
than by the full allowance on paper.
