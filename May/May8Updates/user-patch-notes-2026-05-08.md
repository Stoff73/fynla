# What's new on Fynla — 8 May 2026

## You can now choose how you leave Fynla

We've rebuilt how account deletion works so you have control and peace of mind, and so we meet our regulatory obligations cleanly.

### Two clear options

In **Settings → Privacy → Manage Account Deletion** you'll now see two choices:

- **Delete my Data** — keeps your account active but resets your dashboard so you can start your financial plan again from scratch.
- **Delete my Account** — closes your account entirely.

Both options walk you through a three-step wizard: choose, verify your email with a one-time code, and type a confirmation phrase before anything happens.

### If you've paid for a plan

If you choose **Delete my Account** while your subscription is still running, your account is now **scheduled** for deletion at the end of your current billing period — so you keep the access you've paid for. A banner appears at the top of every page showing the date, with a one-click **Cancel scheduled deletion** button if you change your mind.

### If you change your mind after deletion

If you fully deleted your account and then sign in again, Fynla now recognises you and offers to **restore your account**. Click **Restore my account**, pick a plan, and you're back where you left off.

### Reminders along the way

While your account is scheduled for deletion you'll receive friendly email reminders so you have plenty of warning before the date arrives — and a confirmation email when each step happens.

### Why we keep some data after you go

When your account is fully closed, your record is kept securely for **7 years** to meet UK financial-regulation and GDPR requirements (FCA record-keeping rules). After that period it's automatically and permanently purged — you don't need to do anything.

## Smaller fixes

- **Faster, fresher pages.** We tightened the cache rules so the app always serves you the latest version of pages and data — no more stale dashboards after we ship updates.
- **Cleaner sign-in handling.** If your session expires while you're using the app, we now send you back to the right login screen straight away, with no detours.
- **After-restore experience.** When you restore a deleted account, you now land directly on your dashboard with the **Choose Your Plan** options ready to go.
