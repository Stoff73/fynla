# What's New in Fynla — 23 April 2026

A handful of fixes and improvements to pensions, investments, and how you move around the app.

## What's changed

### Your pension projection works properly from the very first pension you add

Previously, if you added your first pension — either manually or through Fyn — the dashboard would show the pension's value correctly but the projected growth chart would stay at £0. You'd see your £50,000 pot, but the "Projected Value" card and the Monte Carlo chart would sit flat at zero, making it look like your pension was going nowhere.

That's fixed. The projection now updates as soon as you add or change a pension, and the chart shows realistic probability bands from day one.

### One simpler form for adding any pension

When you click **Add Pension** you now go straight to a single form instead of first picking between three tiles (Money Purchase / Final Salary / State Pension). The pension type is the first dropdown on the form, and it covers everything:

- Occupational (Workplace)
- Self-Invested Personal Pension (SIPP)
- Personal Pension
- Stakeholder Pension
- Final Salary (Defined Benefit)
- State Pension

The form shows the right fields for whichever type you pick. One less click and a flow that's the same no matter what kind of pension you're adding.

### Investment account projection now loads

Clicking into one of your investment accounts showed "Failed to load projection data" where the Monte Carlo chart should be. The chart now loads correctly — current value, projected value at 80% confidence, and the 5/10/20/30-year probability bands.

### A calmer, more consistent navigation

- **Top nav stays pinned** as you scroll. No more scrolling all the way back up to reach the menu.
- **The duplicated tabs row is gone.** Each module's section now lives in the left sidebar only, which removes a redundant second navigation layer.
- **Add / Upload buttons are next to the data they act on.** On Retirement and Investments they sit underneath the list of accounts, next to the projection chart. On Property, Liabilities, Personal Valuables, Business, Trusts and Goals they sit at the top-right of the list.
- **Browser tab label** now just reads "Fynla" on every screen, rather than sometimes saying "Sign In — Fynla" after you've already signed in.

## Nothing's changed about your data

Your pensions, investments, goals, and every other record are stored and displayed exactly as before. These are cosmetic and projection-calculation improvements — no records moved, renamed, or were deleted.

## If something still looks odd

If a pension projection on your dashboard still shows £0 after this update, open the pension (click the card), make any small edit and save — that triggers a fresh recalculation. If you'd rather not touch the data, it'll sort itself out within 24 hours on its own.
