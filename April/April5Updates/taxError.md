ERROR 2 — Statutory Sick Pay: Qualification Rules Section: Statutory Sick Pay 
Issue: Document states "You must earn at least £129 per week to qualify." This is no longer correct. The Employment Rights Act 2025 (in force from 6 April 2026) makes two structural changes the document omits entirely:
The Lower Earnings Limit is abolished — all eligible employees now qualify regardless of earnings
The three waiting days are abolished — SSP is now payable from day one of sickness absence
Payment for lower earners is now the lower of the flat rate (£123.25) or 80% of normal weekly earnings
Fix: Remove the £129 earnings qualifier. Add a note explaining day-one entitlement, LEL removal, and the 80% earnings-linked rate for lower earners.
