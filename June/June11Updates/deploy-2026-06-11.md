## Session 2 incident: route:cache shadowed the public homepage (the "/m landing regression")

**Symptom (CSJ report):** the /m pathway landing pages regressed to the old design (PR #521's save-tax teaser + CTA missing).

**Root cause:** `php artisan optimize` (run in both session-1 and session-2 deploy chains per DEPLOY.md) caches routes; under the compiled matcher the SPA catch-all shadows the server-rendered `/` route, so guests — and the /m iframe, which loads `/` — got the bare SPA shell (`app.blade.php` → Vue `LandingPage.vue`, pre-#521 design). The files on disk were always current; only routing was stale. OPcache ruled out (disabled); direct FPM include of `pages/index.php` rendered current content.

**Fix applied:** `php artisan route:clear` on csjones (config stays cached). DEPLOY.md, both build.sh instruction blocks, CLAUDE.md Troubleshooting, and memory updated: NEVER `optimize`/`route:cache` on this app; deploy chains end with explicit `config:cache`; smoke tests must grep content, not just check 200.

**Verified live (Playwright + curl):**
- Logged-out `/` and the `Sec-Fetch-Dest: iframe` phone variant both serve the server-rendered homepage ("Get started for free" present, short title).
- /m logged-out shows the real homepage in-frame: hero, "Get started for free" CTA, "up to £X" save-tax teaser visible, savetax link present.
- Savetax campaign funnel inside /m: clicked through — step 1 (employment status) → answers → step 2 of 4 advances. Campaign logic, onboarding, auth flows untouched by the fix (routing-layer only).
- /pricing serves #521's "Up to 3 savings accounts…" copy.
- Prod (fynla.org) checked: NOT affected (serves the server-rendered homepage).
