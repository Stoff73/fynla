Primary Version

Chris Slater-Jones — Founder, Fynla

I've spent over 20 years in financial services — advising families, managing wealth, and overseeing compliance. That work has taken me across the UK and South Africa, from the advisory front line to the boardroom.

I started in UK banking, then moved into independent financial advice. I hold a Post Graduate Diploma in Financial Planning from the University of the Free State and the Diploma in Regulated Financial Planning (DipPFS) from the Chartered Insurance Institute. I've been FCA-registered and have held senior approved functions including CF30 (customer-facing advisory), SMF3 (Executive Director), and SMF17 (Money Laundering Reporting Officer). My FCA reference is CJS01279, and my record is clean — no disciplinary or regulatory action of any kind.

At St. James's Place Wealth Management, I advised clients as an Associate Partner Practice, specialising in expat financial planning for individuals returning to the UK from South Africa and Australia. I ran my own consultancy, CSJones, focused on reducing complexity and delivering clear outcomes. As a director, I oversaw regulatory compliance, staff training, and anti-money-laundering controls. Through MIRIAD GROUP, I worked across capital markets, investor relations, corporate communications, and ESG — helping companies communicate their investment story.

Through all of this, the same frustration kept returning: the tools families need for proper financial planning were too expensive, too complex, or locked behind professional gatekeepers.

That's why I built Fynla.

Fynla is a UK-focused financial planning platform for individuals and families — not institutions. It covers protection, savings, investments, retirement, and estate planning across five integrated modules, all built around current UK tax rules, ISA allowances, pension limits, and inheritance tax thresholds. It includes spouse-linking for joint finances, Monte Carlo retirement projections, and a Letter to Spouse — an auto-populated document of essential information for your loved ones.

Quality financial planning tools should be accessible to everyone, not just those who can afford a professional adviser. Fynla is the platform I always wished my clients had.
