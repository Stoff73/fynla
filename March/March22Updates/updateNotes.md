# Fynla Update Notes — 20-22 March 2026

## Onboarding

- Your journey now resumes where you left off instead of starting from the beginning
- You can click any step in the progress bar to jump directly to it
- New expenditure step added to all journeys so you can record your monthly spending
- New debts step added to two journeys so you can record loans, credit cards, and other liabilities
- A top navigation bar now shows the Fynla logo and "Your Journey" heading during onboarding
- Skipping the goals step now shows a friendly confirmation instead of an error
- Switching tabs on the assets step now closes any open forms
- Premium Bonds and NS&I Savings forms no longer show irrelevant fields
- Required fields on the family member form now have red asterisks
- Pension forms for personal and workplace pensions now ask for your planned access age
- Property forms for leasehold properties now ask for the expiry date and calculate remaining years automatically
- Expenditure fields now show correctly as whole pounds
- "Partner" is shown instead of "Spouse" when you are single
- Family members cards no longer overlap on smaller screens
- Useful resources moved to a sidebar card for easier access

## Will Builder

- Your will draft now saves automatically and resumes at the correct step when you return
- A success screen appears when you complete your will with options to view signing instructions or return to estate planning
- The will dashboard now shows your full will details including beneficiaries, gifts, funeral wishes, and executors
- A "View Will" button lets you review your completed will at any time
- You can now add multiple executors to your will

## Liabilities Dashboard

- New liabilities page accessible from the sidebar under Finances
- Shows all your debts in one place including personal loans, credit cards, and mortgages from your properties
- Mortgages display a "Property" badge and link back to the property section for editing
- Filter your liabilities by type including a mortgages filter
- Interest rates now display correctly

## Sidebar Navigation

- All menu items are now always visible under their section headings
- No items are hidden based on your journey stage

## Dashboard

- Removed the "Suggested for You" goals card

## Fyn Assistant

- Fyn now navigates you to the correct page when you ask to see something
- Fyn knows about your goals and life events and can reference them by name
- Fyn can create financial records for you by filling in forms visually with a highlight animation for protection policies, investments and money purchase pensions. I still have to map the following: DB pension, property, mortgage, estate assets/gifts, trusts, business interests, chattels, goals, life events, family members, edit flow.
- Chat history is now accessible from the docked chat panel
- Fyn's responses now anchor at the top so you read downward naturally
- When something goes wrong behind the scenes, Fyn answers from general knowledge instead of showing an error

## What-If Scenarios

- Ask Fyn "What if I retire at 55?" and a persistent scenario is created comparing your current position with the projected change
- Scenarios have their own detail page with module-by-module comparisons

## Goals and Life Events

- Behind-schedule banner now shows specific goal names and remaining amounts
- Inline monthly contribution input on goal cards
- Five new life event types: divorce, marriage, new child, job loss, and income change

## Information Guide

- "What powers this view?" panel links now take you directly to the correct page for adding missing data
