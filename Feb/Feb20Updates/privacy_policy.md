## 7. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

### 7.1 What Automated Processing We Perform

Fynla uses automated calculations to provide financial planning outputs including:

- **Risk profiling:** We assess your investment risk profile based on factors including age, income, assets, liabilities, dependants, and time to retirement. This produces a risk level indicator (e.g., lower, medium, higher) used to inform asset allocation guidance.
- **Tax calculations:** We estimate your income tax, National Insurance, capital gains tax, and inheritance tax liabilities using current HMRC rates and thresholds.
- **Retirement projections:** We model future pension income and fund values using actuarial life tables, assumed growth rates, and inflation.
- **Protection needs analysis:** We calculate recommended life insurance and income protection cover based on your financial circumstances and dependants.
- **Estate planning estimates:** We estimate inheritance tax liability and the effect of various estate planning strategies.

### 7.2 The Logic Involved

All calculations use publicly available UK tax rates, actuarial life tables published by the Office for National Statistics, standard financial modelling techniques, and assumptions that you can view and adjust within the Service.

### 7.3 Significance and Consequences

These automated outputs are **informational only**. They are designed to help you understand your financial position and explore planning options. They do not:
- Constitute regulated financial advice
- Make any binding decisions about your financial products or services
- Affect your access to financial services
- Determine your eligibility for any product or service

You are always free to disregard the outputs and make your own financial decisions. No decisions with legal or similarly significant effects are made solely by automated processing.

### 7.4 Your Rights

Under UK GDPR Article 22, you have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects. As our automated outputs are informational and do not produce such effects, Article 22 does not apply. However, if you have concerns about any automated output, please contact us and we will review it manually.

## 11. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data. You can exercise most of these rights directly through the Service, or by contacting us at privacy@fynla.org.

### 11.1 Right of Access (Article 15)

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. You can exercise this right by:
- **Self-service:** Using the data export feature in your account settings, which generates a downloadable file containing all your personal data in JSON or CSV format
- **Formal request:** Contacting us at privacy@fynla.org

We will respond to formal requests within one calendar month. This may be extended by a further two months for complex or numerous requests, in which case we will inform you within the first month.

### 11.2 Right to Rectification (Article 16)

You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected. You can update your personal information at any time through the Service. If you are unable to correct data yourself, contact us and we will rectify it promptly.

### 11.3 Right to Erasure (Article 17)

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data. You can exercise this right by:
- **Data-only deletion:** Deleting all financial data while keeping your account active, through your account settings
- **Full account deletion:** Permanently deleting your account and all associated data, through your account settings

Full account deletion is a verified three-step process requiring identity confirmation (via multi-factor authentication or email verification code) and explicit confirmation to protect against unauthorised deletion.

There are limited circumstances where we may not be able to comply with an erasure request, including where retention is necessary for:
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- The establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims
- GDPR audit logs required to demonstrate compliance (retained for 7 years)

### 11.4 Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18)

You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances, including where:
- You contest the accuracy of the data (restricted while we verify)
- Processing is unlawful but you prefer restriction over erasure
- We no longer need the data but you need it for legal claims
- You have objected to processing (restricted pending verification of our grounds)

To request restriction, contact us at privacy@fynla.org.

### 11.5 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. You can exercise this right using the data export feature in your account settings, which provides your data in JSON or CSV format. You may also request that we transmit your data directly to another controller where technically feasible.

### 11.6 Right to Object (Article 21)

You have the right to object to processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as the lawful basis. Upon receiving your objection, we will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or the processing is necessary for legal claims.

**Direct marketing:** You have an absolute right to object to processing for direct marketing purposes at any time. We will cease marketing communications immediately upon receiving your objection.

To exercise your right to object, contact us at privacy@fynla.org or withdraw marketing consent through your account settings.

### 11.7 Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7(3))

Where we process your data based on consent (marketing communications and health-related data), you have the right to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You can withdraw consent by:

## 13. Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

### 13.1 Technical Measures

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Encryption at rest** | Sensitive financial fields are encrypted using AES-256-CBC encryption. Multi-factor authentication secrets are encrypted before storage. |
| **Encryption in transit** | All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). HSTS headers enforce HTTPS connections. |
| **Password security** | Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a work factor of 12. We never store passwords in plain text. Passwords must meet complexity requirements (minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, digit, and special character). |
| **Multi-factor authentication** | Optional TOTP-based multi-factor authentication using authenticator apps. Recovery codes are individually hashed using bcrypt. |
| **Progressive lockout** | Repeated failed login attempts trigger progressive account lockout (from 1 minute to 24 hours). IP-based rate limiting blocks excessive failed attempts. |
| **Session security** | Sessions are encrypted. Cookies are HTTP-only (not accessible to JavaScript), use SameSite protections, and are encrypted by the application. Users can view and revoke active sessions. |
| **Content Security Policy** | CSP headers restrict the sources from which content can be loaded, mitigating cross-site scripting attacks. |
| **Security headers** | X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options, X-XSS-Protection, and Referrer-Policy headers are applied to all responses. |
| **Input sanitisation** | All user input is sanitised to prevent cross-site scripting and injection attacks. |
| **Rate limiting** | API endpoints are rate-limited to prevent abuse. Sensitive operations (password changes, data erasure) have stricter limits. |
| **CORS restrictions** | Cross-origin requests are restricted to authorised origins only. |
| **API authentication** | All API requests require Bearer token authentication. Tokens expire after 8 hours. Token prefix enables automated secret scanning detection. |

### 13.2 Organisational Measures

| Measure | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Access control** | Role-based access control limits who can access user data. Administrative functions require elevated permissions. |
| **Audit trail** | All data access and modifications are logged with timestamps, IP addresses, and user identifiers. |
| **Data minimisation** | We collect only the data necessary for financial planning. Optional fields can be left blank without affecting core functionality. |
| **Regular review** | Security measures are regularly reviewed and updated. |

### 13.3 Data Breach Procedures

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will:
1. Notify the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
2. Notify you without undue delay if the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms
3. Document all breaches, including those that do not require notification, in our breach register
