## 2. The instruments, verbatim

Everything below is quoted, not paraphrased. All read **2026-08-21**, "latest available
(revised)".

**Mental Capacity Act 2005 (2005 c. 9)**

- **s.9(1)** — *"A lasting power of attorney is a power of attorney under which the donor ("P") confers on the donee (or donees) authority to make decisions about all or any of the following—(a) P's personal welfare or specified matters concerning P's personal welfare, and (b) P's property and affairs or specified matters concerning P's property and affairs, and which includes authority to make such decisions in circumstances where P no longer has capacity."*
- **s.10(1)** — *"A donee of a lasting power of attorney must be—(a) an individual who has reached 18, or (b) if the power relates only to P's property and affairs, either such an individual or a trust corporation."*
- **s.10(2)** — *"An individual who is bankrupt or is a person in relation to whom a debt relief order is made may not be appointed as donee of a lasting power of attorney in relation to P's property and affairs."*
- **s.10(4)** — *"The instrument may appoint them to act—(a) jointly, (b) jointly and severally, or (c) jointly in respect of some matters and jointly and severally in respect of others."*
- **s.10(5)** — *"To the extent to which it does not specify whether they are to act jointly or jointly and severally, the instrument is to be assumed to appoint them to act jointly."*
- **s.10(6)** — *"If they are to act jointly, a failure, as respects one of them, to comply with the requirements of subsection (1) or (2) or Part 1 or 2 of Schedule 1 prevents a lasting power of attorney from being created."*
- **s.10(7)** — *"If they are to act jointly and severally, a failure, as respects one of them, to comply with the requirements of subsection (1) or (2) or Part 1 or 2 of Schedule 1—(a) prevents the appointment taking effect in his case, but (b) does not prevent a lasting power of attorney from being created in the case of the other or others."*
- **s.10(8)** — *"An instrument used to create a lasting power of attorney—(a) cannot give the donee (or, if more than one, any of them) power to appoint a substitute or successor, but (b) may itself appoint a person to replace the donee (or, if more than one, any of them) on the occurrence of an event mentioned in section 13(6)(a) to (d) which has the effect of terminating the donee's appointment."*
- **s.11(7)** — *"Where a lasting power of attorney authorises the donee (or, if more than one, any of them) to make decisions about P's personal welfare, the authority—(a) does not extend to making such decisions in circumstances other than those where P lacks, or the donee reasonably believes that P lacks, capacity, (b) is subject to sections 24 to 26 (advance decisions to refuse treatment), and (c) extends to giving or refusing consent to the carrying out or continuation of a treatment by a person providing health care for P."*
- **s.13(5)** — *"The occurrence in relation to a donee of an event mentioned in subsection (6)—(a) terminates his appointment, and (b) except in the cases given in subsection (7), revokes the power."*
- **s.13(6)** — *"The events are—(a) the disclaimer of the appointment by the donee …, (b) subject to subsections (8) and (9), the death or bankruptcy of the donee or the making of a debt relief order … in respect of the donee or, if the donee is a trust corporation, its winding-up or dissolution, (c) subject to subsection (11), the dissolution or annulment of a marriage or civil partnership between the donor and the donee, (d) the lack of capacity of the donee."*
- **s.13(7)** — *"The cases are—(a) the donee is replaced under the terms of the instrument, (b) he is one of two or more persons appointed to act as donees jointly and severally in respect of any matter and, after the event, there is at least one remaining donee."*
- **s.13(8)** — *"The bankruptcy of a donee or the making of a debt relief order … does not terminate his appointment, or revoke the power, in so far as his authority relates to P's personal welfare."*
- **s.13(9)** — *"Where the donee is bankrupt merely because an interim bankruptcy restrictions order has effect in respect of him … his appointment and the power are suspended, so far as they relate to P's property and affairs, for so long as the order has effect."*
- **s.13(11)** — *"The dissolution or annulment of a marriage or civil partnership does not terminate the appointment of a donee, or revoke the power, if the instrument provided that it was not to do so."*
- **Sch 1 para 2(6)** — *"The certificate may not be given by a person appointed as donee."* (established W-0100; commencement now checked)

**Lasting Powers of Attorney, Enduring Powers of Attorney and Public Guardian Regulations 2007 (S.I. 2007/1253)**

- **reg 8(1)** — *"Subject to paragraph (3), the following persons may give an LPA certificate—(a) a person chosen by the donor as being someone who has known him personally for the period of at least two years which ends immediately before the date on which that person signs the LPA certificate; (b) a person chosen by the donor who, on account of his professional skills and expertise, reasonably considers that he is competent to make the judgments necessary to certify the matters set out in paragraph (2)(1)(e) of Schedule 1 to the Act."*
- **reg 8(2)** — *"The following are examples of persons within paragraph (1)(b)—(a) a registered health care professional; (b) a barrister, solicitor or advocate called or admitted in any part of the United Kingdom; (c) a registered social worker; or (d) an independent mental capacity advocate."*
- **reg 8(3)** — disqualifies, in respect of any instrument intended to create a lasting power of attorney: *"(a) a family member of the donor; (b) a donee of that power; (c) a donee of—(i) any other lasting power of attorney, or (ii) an enduring power of attorney, which has been executed by the donor (whether or not it has been revoked); (d) a family member of a donee within sub-paragraph (b); (e) a director or employee of a trust corporation acting as a donee within sub-paragraph (b); (f) a business partner or employee of—(i) the donor, or (ii) a donee within sub-paragraph (b); (g) an owner, director, manager or employee of any care home in which the donor is living when the instrument is executed; or (h) a family member of a person within sub-paragraph (g)."*
- **reg 8(4)** — defines *care home*, *registered health care professional*, *registered social worker*. **Does not define "family member".**

**The Public Guardian (Fees, etc) Regulations 2007 (S.I. 2007/2051), the Schedule** —
lasting power of attorney application **£92**; repeat application **£46**; office copy
**£35.00**.

**Office of the Public Guardian, published guidance** (`gov.uk/power-of-attorney/register`)
— *"Applying to register an LPA costs £92."* · *"It takes 8 to 10 weeks to register an
LPA if there are no mistakes in the application."* **No last-updated date displayed on
the page.**

## 4. What I changed my mind about, and what I refused

**Recorded because a ruling with no friction in it is usually a ruling nobody tested.**

- **W-0103 conflict 2 (donor as own certificate provider).** I expected to close this by
  citation and **could not**. reg 8(3) lists eight disqualifications — including the
  donor's *family members* — and **the donor is not among them**. Combined with reg 8(1)'s

## 5. Found while sourcing — routed, not filed

**I hold no board block, so none of these is an item.** All three go to team-lead with a
recommendation. Each is a **product gap that Fynla's own wording actively worsens**,
which is why they are not merely "more things the law says".

### 5.1 reg 8(3) disqualifies seven more categories, and Fynla points users at one of them

**`checkCertificateProviderKnownYears()` presents two years' acquaintance as *the*
requirement.** A donor's most likely two-years-known person is their spouse or their
child — and **reg 8(3)(a) disqualifies a family member of the donor.** Fynla is not
merely silent; its wording steers.

**Two of the eight are detectable from data Fynla already holds:**

- **reg 8(3)(b)** — a donee of *that* power. This is W-0102, being built.
- **reg 8(3)(c)** — a donee of **any other** lasting or enduring power of attorney executed
  by the donor. **Fynla holds both instrument types for one user**, so "the health and
  welfare attorney certifies the property instrument" is a state the application can
  construct *and* can detect. Nothing checks it.

The remaining six (family of the donor, family of an attorney, trust-corporation staff,
business partners and employees, care-home staff, their family) are not derivable and
belong in `NOT_CHECKED`. **Recommendation:** one item, sequenced with W-0102/W-0103 —
same file, same check, and the two-year framing is the misleading part.

### 5.2 Divorce terminates the appointment, and the instrument can opt out

**s.13(6)(c)** puts *"the dissolution or annulment of a marriage or civil partnership
between the donor and the donee"* on the list of events that terminate the appointment.
The commonest attorney is a spouse. **s.13(11)** then provides that it does **not**
terminate *"if the instrument provided that it was not to do so."*

**That is an election the donor can make in the instrument. Fynla's wizard does not offer
it and the generated document is silent on it.** Same shape as W-0108 — a statutory
feature of the instrument the document does not mention — but a wizard gap rather than
check wording, so it does not belong in W-0107. **Recommendation:** its own item, after
W-0108, since W-0108 settles how a not-a-choice statement is written and this one needs
the same treatment plus a field.

### 5.3 `PowerOfAttorneyTab.vue:105` may not be the same kind of surface as the other two

W-0109's three sites are treated as interchangeable. Two are wizard and check copy. **If
that tab is reachable before purchase, the £92 is a claim made to acquire a customer** and
lands in the consumer-protection row of the regime map (`05-perimeter.md` §1.1 — DMCC Act
2024 Part 4, **Unmapped**, and its Chapter 2 commencement could not be pinned).
**I have not checked reachability.** It does not change the wording; flagged so nobody

## 7. §6 questions — written, not answered

Per §1.3 rule 4 and §7.4. **These are not agent-answerable and I have not answered them.**
Each carries the product fact that raised it, so a lawyer can be handed the question with
the content attached rather than asked to review a fintech.

7. **May the donor of a lasting power of attorney give their own certificate?**
   S.I. 2007/1253 reg 8(3) lists eight disqualifications and the donor is not among them,
   while reg 8(1) frames the provider as *"a person chosen by the donor"* and Sch 1 para
   2(1)(e) requires the certificate to be about the donor's own understanding and freedom
   from pressure. **Product fact:** `certificate_provider_name` is free text and nothing
   compares it to `donor_full_name`, so Fynla can generate this instrument today.

8. **May the donor be appointed as a donee of their own lasting power of attorney?**
   s.9(1) and s.10(1) frame the relationship and state what a donee must be; neither
   excludes the donor in terms. **Product fact:** `donor_full_name` is never compared to
   any `lpa_attorneys.full_name`, and the rendered document reads "I, [name] … appoint the
   attorney(s) named below: Attorney 1: [same name]".

9. **What is a "family member" for S.I. 2007/1253 reg 8(3)(a) and (d)?** reg 8(4) defines
   *care home*, *registered health care professional* and *registered social worker*, and
   **does not define "family member"**. **Product fact:** Fynla holds `relationship_to_donor`
   on `lpa_attorneys` and holds spouse links and `FamilyMember` rows, so the scope of the
   term determines whether a check is buildable at all or whether this is disclosure-only.

10. **Does generating a Lasting Power of Attorney, and reporting checks over it, carry
    exposure beyond the reserved-activity question W-0100 answered?** W-0100 established
    that LSA 2007 Sch 2 para 5(3)(c) excludes powers of attorney from reserved instrument
    activities, and stopped there — *not reserved ≠ permitted*. **Product fact:** the tool
    now also states what the Mental Capacity Act provides (W-0108) and reports checks
    against statutory requirements (W-0102, W-0104, W-0106), which is more than it did
    when W-0100 was written.

**Numbered 7–10 to continue `05-perimeter.md` §6's existing six. I have not edited §6** —
the trunk is the Archivist's and amendments are gated.
