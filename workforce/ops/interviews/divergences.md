# Divergence Register

Where a founder's interview answer conflicts with an already-ratified trunk clause.

**The clause stands unchanged until a meeting resolves it.** Nothing here amends
doctrine. Divergences are recorded, not merged — two plausible answers averaged
into one produces doctrine neither founder holds, and nobody notices until the
Chief of Staff judges work against it.

Governed by `core/registry/people.md` §4.
